Who I Am

Krish Bakriwala
AI Engineer · Data Scientist
MSc AI Brandenburg UTech Germany

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Brandenburg University of Technology in Germany, with a background in Computer Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, data science, and applied AI engineering — I care about building things that actually work, not just notebooks that look good.

On the data science side, I have experience in experiment design, EDA on real messy datasets, statistical validation, predictive modelling with XGBoost and ensemble methods, feature engineering, and building analytical dashboards with Power BI. I've worked with imbalanced classification problems, time-series forecasting, and model evaluation frameworks.

On the AI engineering side, I build RAG pipelines, LLM-powered tools, and backend services — using FastAPI, Docker, PostgreSQL, and tools like LangChain, Groq, and ChromaDB. I focus on clean architecture, reproducibility, and honest evaluation — not just impressive-sounding demos.

🌍 Open to internships and working student roles in Machine Learning, Data Science, AI Engineering, and Backend Development across Europe — particularly Germany. Available from August 2026.
