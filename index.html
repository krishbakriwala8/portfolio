Background

About me

I'm a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Brandenburg University of Technology (BTU) in Cottbus, with a Bachelor's in Computer Engineering from Gujarat Technological University.

On the data science side I work on experiment design, EDA on messy data, statistical testing, feature engineering and ensemble models — and I pick metrics that fit the business problem, like the real cost matrix in my predictive-maintenance project rather than raw accuracy.

On the AI engineering side I build RAG pipelines, LangGraph multi-agent systems, MCP tool integrations and LLM evaluation frameworks, served through FastAPI with PostgreSQL, tests and CI.

I'm especially interested in applied AI for industry — manufacturing, automotive and energy — where models have to be explainable, reliable and connected to real systems.
