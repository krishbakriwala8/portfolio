About Me

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Brandenburg University of Technology (BTU) in Cottbus, Germany, with an undergraduate background in Computer Engineering from Gujarat Technological University.

My work covers both sides of the field. On the data science side — experiment design, EDA on messy real-world data, statistical testing, predictive modelling with ensemble methods, feature engineering, and analytical dashboards. I care about honest evaluation: the right metrics for the problem, not the most flattering numbers.

On the AI engineering side — RAG pipelines, LLM evaluation frameworks, agentic systems, and production backend services using FastAPI, Docker, and PostgreSQL. I build things that actually run in production, not just notebooks.

Previously worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at an AI analytics startup (2023) and as an ML intern at NatRIX Software (2022) — both in India, before moving to Germany for the M.Sc.
